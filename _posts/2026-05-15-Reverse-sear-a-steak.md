The reverse sear is:

1. **Low oven** (225–275°F) on a wire rack until the internal temp is about 10–15°F below your target (e.g., 115–120°F for medium-rare)
2. **Rest briefly**, then **rip-hot sear** in a cast iron or carbon steel pan, 60–90 seconds per side

Concept: Put it on a rack so air circulates; the pan goes into a low oven, not a hot one. The sear happens at the end on the stovetop, not in the oven.

The advantage over traditional sear-first: the low-and-slow phase dries the surface, so you get a dramatically better crust when you sear, and the interior is edge-to-edge even doneness with no gray band.
