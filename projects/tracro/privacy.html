Privacy Policy for Tracro

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Tracro is a local-first calorie and macronutrient tracker for iPhone.

Data Collection

Tracro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your food logs, nutrition history, goals, favorites, barcode mappings, or meal templates with Benson Labs.

Local Data

Nutrition data created or saved in Tracro is stored locally on your device. Tracro does not require an account.

Backups and Files

Tracro can export a portable backup file when you choose to do so. The file leaves the app only through the system file tools and the destination you select, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Imported backups are read from the file you select.

Food Lookup

When you request a food lookup, Tracro may use Open Food Facts to retrieve publicly available product information. Tracro does not send your nutrition history or account information as part of that lookup.

Analytics and Tracking

Tracro does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking technology.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Tracro’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the latest revision.
