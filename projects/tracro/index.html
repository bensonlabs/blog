Tracro Support

Tracro is a local-first calorie and macronutrient tracker for iPhone.

Getting Started

Create foods, set your nutrition goals, and log meals from the Today screen. You can also scan a barcode or look up publicly available food information when adding a food.

Features

Tracro supports calorie and macronutrient tracking, daily history, favorites, barcode mappings, and reusable meal templates.

Backup and Restore

Open Settings and Data Management to create a portable backup or restore one you previously exported. Tracro shows backup details before a restore and keeps the previous data available for recovery when applicable.
