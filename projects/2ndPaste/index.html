Privacy Policy for 2ndPaste Effective date: [DATE] 2ndPaste is a local clipboard-history utility for macOS. Data Collection 2ndPaste does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Clipboard Data Clipboard items processed by 2ndPaste remain on your Mac. Clipboard history is stored locally in memory and is cleared when you quit the application. 2ndPaste does not upload clipboard contents to a server or synchronize them through a cloud service. Analytics and Tracking 2ndPaste does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technology, or third-party telemetry. Accounts 2ndPaste does not require or provide user accounts. Permissions 2ndPaste uses macOS clipboard access to monitor and restore clipboard items. The Mac App Store edition does not request Accessibility access or synthesize keyboard input. User Controls You can pause clipboard monitoring or clear all clipboard history from the application’s controls at any time. Quitting 2ndPaste also clears its current in-memory history. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated if 2ndPaste’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the latest revision.
