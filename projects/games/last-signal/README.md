# The Last Signal

A text-based adventure. You are Dr. Mara Chen, a lone scientist investigating an abandoned research station after receiving a fragmented distress signal from deep within the facility.

## The Premise

The station hums with malfunctioning equipment and an eerie, pulsing signal coming from somewhere deeper. Your mission: explore, uncover what happened, and make a choice that will define everything.

Three acts. One station. Two endings.

## How to Play

1. Read the room descriptions
2. Click options to explore, collect items, and navigate between rooms
3. Use found items to unlock new areas
4. Make your final choice

No keyboard required. Just click your way through the dark.
