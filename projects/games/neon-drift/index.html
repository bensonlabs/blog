NEON DRIFT

Primary Objective

Survive as long as possible in the Neon Drift Zone. Your ship auto-accelerates; manage momentum, drift through tight formations, and graze obstacles to multiply your score.

Hull Integrity

Collisions deal 15% damage. Hull does not regenerate. At 0% the run ends.

Boost System

Hold Boost to engage Afterburner (2.2x thrust). Drains Boost Meter. Recharges slowly when released. Strategic boosting is key for escape vectors.

Keyboard / Gamepad

  • ← / A Steer Left
  • → / D Steer Right
  • ↑ / W / Space Afterburner
  • Shift Drift Brake (Hold)
  • Esc Pause / Menu

Touch / Mouse

On-screen buttons appear automatically on mobile. Click the ≡ button (top-right) or press Esc to access Menu/Reset during play.

Drift Technique

Enter a turn at high speed → Hold Drift → Turn sharply → Release Drift to grip and shoot forward. Preserves lateral momentum.

Base Score

Accrues passively based on Speed × Time. Faster = more points/sec.

Near Miss Bonus

Pass within 60px of an obstacle without hitting: +50 × Multiplier.
High risk, high reward. Essential for leaderboard runs.

Multiplier

Starts at x1.0. Near misses increase it (Max x10.0). Decays rapidly without near misses. Maintain the chain!
