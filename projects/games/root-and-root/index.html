Notes from the rooftop

Make the weather useful.

Every plant needs a particular number of drops. A shower only brings so many. Your awnings are the plumbing.

  1. Slide the middle. Move an awning along its rail by dragging its numbered center. It can move even when holding water.
  2. Pull a tip. Drag either end up or down. Level awnings hold their drops; tilted awnings pour off the lower end. Water can fall onto another awning.
  3. Give every pot its share. Its meter shows how much it still needs. A full pot cannot use extra water, so aim for another plant.

There is no time limit. Pause to make adjustments. The dotted line previews where water will fall. After the shower, any water on your awnings is still yours to use.

Earn three leaves by spilling at most two drops, two leaves for spilling at most one fifth of the forecast, or one leaf for getting the garden watered. You can play any of the seven showers in any order. Your best results are saved on this device when browser storage is available.
